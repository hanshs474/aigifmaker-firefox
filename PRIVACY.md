# Privacy policy — AI GIF Maker for Firefox

Last updated: 2026-11-10

The AI GIF Maker Firefox add-on ("the extension") is published by AI GIF Maker (https://aigifmaker.ai/). Contact: support@aigifmaker.ai.

**What is stored, and where.** Your AI GIF Maker API key and a list of your most recent GIFs (up to 20: the prompt text, the task id, status and the GIF URL) are stored only in your browser with `storage.local`. They are never sent to the developer or any third party. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

**What is sent, and to whom.** Only when you choose the menu item or press "Make GIF": the text you selected (or typed in the popup) is sent to `https://aigifmaker.ai/api/ai/generate`, with your API key in the `Authorization` header. To show progress, the task id is sent to `https://aigifmaker.ai/api/ai/query`; to show your credits, an empty request is sent to `https://aigifmaker.ai/api/user/get-user-credits`. The extension talks to no other server and contains no analytics, ads or remote code.

**What is not collected.** Page contents, browsing history, cookies, or any text you did not select and send yourself.

How AI GIF Maker handles your prompts, GIFs and account: https://aigifmaker.ai/privacy-policy
